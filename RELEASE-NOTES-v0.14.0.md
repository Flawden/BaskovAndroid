# BaskovAndroid v0.14.0 — My Music & Servers

BaskovAndroid now starts to look like a complete Baskov Music client instead of a collection of playback screens.

## My Music
- first-class personal Favorites screen;
- shared Android ↔ Discord favorite persistence;
- search and add remote tracks;
- remove, clear and play all favorites;
- add the current remote Now Playing track to favorites;
- local phone files remain intentionally local-only.

## Servers
- list every Discord guild accessible to the paired identity;
- show read-only current track/session/queue status per server;
- switch the active Android guild from the hub;
- no remote Discord voice/player mutation permissions are introduced yet.

## Home
Search, Favorites, Playlists, Servers, local music, history and recommendations are now presented as first-class destinations.
